Based in Kigali

Rimba Creative Media is a creative agency based in Kigali, Rwanda. We help individuals and businesses create powerful visual content that gets noticed — from brand photography and video production to full digital marketing campaigns.

We also run a training school where we teach the next generation of creatives. Our 3-month courses in photography, video production, and web development are practical, affordable, and taught by people who do this work every day.

Whether you need us to market your business, shoot your event, or teach your team — we're here for it. Come work with us.
